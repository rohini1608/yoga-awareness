01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small practices.
Meaningful change.

Yoga is a holistic practice that brings together physical postures, breathing techniques, relaxation and mindfulness.

This Community Service Project focuses on creating awareness about the value of yoga and encouraging people in the community to include safe, simple practices in their everyday routines.

The project aims to share accessible information, demonstrate beginner-friendly practices and encourage participants to approach health with consistency and self-awareness.

What we want to achieve

02

Introduce basic postures, breathing and relaxation with attention to individual comfort.

Promote inclusive sessions and invite community members to ask questions and practise.

Encourage regular movement, mindful breathing, good posture and stress-management habits.

03 / WEEK-BY-WEEK

Weekly activities

An eight-week framework for documenting the project journey. Replace or edit each item to match the activities and dates actually completed.

WEEK 01

Introduction and community observation

Understand the community setting, introduce the project, identify participants’ awareness and learn about their interests and needs.

Learning focus: listening, observation and planning.
WEEK 02

Introduction to yoga and its benefits

Explain the meaning of yoga, its main components and the possible benefits of regular, appropriate practice for general well-being.

Learning focus: communicating health information clearly.
WEEK 03

Warm-up and basic standing postures

Introduce gentle warm-up movements and beginner-friendly standing postures, demonstrating alignment and comfortable movement.

Learning focus: demonstration and safe participation.
WEEK 04

Seated and relaxation postures

Discuss comfortable seated positions, simple stretches and relaxation. Encourage participants to avoid forcing any movement.

Learning focus: body awareness and relaxation.
WEEK 05

Breathing practices and mindfulness

Introduce gentle breath awareness and simple calming techniques. Explain that breathing should remain natural and comfortable.

Learning focus: concentration and mindful breathing.
WEEK 06

Guided practice and community interaction

Plan or conduct a guided beginner session, invite questions and adapt the pace to participants’ abilities and comfort.

Learning focus: teamwork and inclusive instruction.
WEEK 07

Feedback and follow-up awareness

Gather participant feedback, review key learning points and discuss practical ways to maintain a consistent routine.

Learning focus: evaluation and community engagement.
WEEK 08

Review, reflection and documentation

Summarize the project, organize photographs and activity records, reflect on learning and identify opportunities for continued practice.

Learning focus: reporting and reflection.

06 / CONCLUSION

Well-being begins
with awareness.

The Yoga Awareness and Practice project highlights the importance of sharing reliable information and making healthy practices accessible to the community. Through awareness, demonstrations, interaction and reflection, the project seeks to encourage people to make thoughtful choices for their physical and mental well-being.

The lasting value of the project is not limited to a single session. It lies in helping people understand that simple, safe and regular habits can become part of everyday life.
